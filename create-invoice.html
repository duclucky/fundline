Why invoice in USDC on Fundline

Non-custodial

Funds move directly from your client to you. Fundline never holds or can withdraw your money.

On-chain proof

Every paid invoice has a verifiable settlement on Arc, so there is no dispute about whether you were paid.

Low, predictable fees

USDC is the gas token on Arc, so transfers are cheap and fast with sub-second finality.

Cross-chain pay

Clients on other chains can bridge USDC in via CCTP and still pay your invoice.

Frequently asked questions

How do I create a USDC invoice?

Connect your wallet on Fundline, add your line items and client details, and share the pay link. Your client pays in USDC and the payment is verified on-chain.

What token and network does Fundline use?

USDC on the Arc blockchain. USDC is the native gas token on Arc, so payments are fast and fees are low and predictable.

Are there fees to create an invoice?

Creating and sharing an invoice is free. Payment goes wallet to wallet directly from your client to you; Fundline never holds your funds.

How does my client pay the invoice?

They open the pay link, connect a wallet or scan a QR, and send USDC. Cross-chain payers can bridge in via CCTP. Fundline verifies the on-chain payment and marks the invoice paid.

Can I get paid without a bank or payment processor?

Yes. Fundline settles directly in USDC on-chain, so there is no bank, no card processor, and no chargebacks.
