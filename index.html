Features

Built for independent work

Payments

The payment goes wallet to wallet

USDC transfers from your client's wallet directly to yours. Nothing passes through Fundline; the contracts hold no balance at any point.

Settlement

Your client pays from any chain

They can pay from Ethereum or Base and the USDC still lands on Arc, real and unwrapped, in one flow. No manual bridging required.

Payroll

Pay your whole team in one transaction

Upload a list of recipients and amounts, then settle everyone at once through the non-custodial batch router. One signature, one payout.

AI Workflows

Tools that do the work, paid per run

Run multi-step AI workflows and pay per use in USDC through an escrow that releases on success and refunds on failure. The result comes back as a clean report.

Agents

Let software pay per request

Software and AI agents can create invoices, receive payment links, and get pinged the moment payment confirms, all via the REST API and x402.

Security

Verified on-chain before marked paid

The paid status is only set after a matching on-chain transaction is found and confirmed. No confirmed transaction, no paid status.

Create an invoice via API

Three things, all in USDC: run AI workflows to produce work like research or proposals, send invoices to get paid by clients across Arc, Ethereum, or Base, and pay your team or contractors in a single bulk transaction. Every payment is verified on-chain, and funds always move wallet to wallet, never through Fundline.

You fund a single run into the FundlineRunEscrow contract from your own wallet. The workflow runs, and on success the payment is released and an on-chain receipt is written. If the run fails, the escrow refunds you. Fundline never holds the money; the contract releases or refunds by rule, and you can verify every step on Arcscan.

Yes. Bulk payout uses the FundlineBatchRouter, which sends USDC from your wallet directly to every recipient in one atomic transaction. The contract holds no balance and has no owner withdrawal path, so the funds are never in Fundline's custody.
