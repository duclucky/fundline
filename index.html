Product

Built for freelancers, indie teams and service agents.

Fundline turns stablecoin payment collection into a clean invoice workflow: create, share, pay, verify, alert, receipt and export.

01 Create invoice

Client details, due date, notes and line items with automatic USDC total.

02 Share payment link

Public /pay/:id page with QR code and receiving wallet details.

03 Verify payment

Arcscan checks payer wallet, merchant wallet, amount and transaction hash.

04 Send receipt

Paid invoices can generate PDF receipts and CSV records.

Payments

Direct wallet settlement

PaymentRouter transfers USDC from payer wallet to merchant wallet. The contract does not hold funds.

Agents

API-first invoice creation

Backends and AI agents can create invoices through protected endpoints and receive payment links.

Security

Verify before paid

The paid state is only saved after a matching on-chain payment is found and persisted on the server.
