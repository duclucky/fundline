How it works

Four steps from invoice to receipt

01 Create invoice

Add client details, due date, line items and notes. The USDC total is calculated automatically.

02 Share payment link

A public /pay/:id page is generated with a QR code and the receiving wallet address.

03 Verify payment

Arcscan checks the payer wallet, merchant wallet, amount and transaction hash on-chain before marking the invoice paid.

04 Send receipt

Paid invoices generate a PDF receipt and CSV record. Telegram alerts fire automatically on confirmation.

Payments

Direct wallet settlement

PaymentRouter transfers USDC from payer wallet to merchant wallet. The contract holds no funds at any point.

Settlement

Agents

API-first invoice creation

Security

Verify before paid

The paid state is only saved after a matching on-chain payment is found and confirmed. No false positives, no premature settlement.

Trust and security

Your funds go directly to your wallet

Fundline is non-custodial by design. The smart contract routes USDC and never holds a balance.

No custody risk

The PaymentRouter contract transfers USDC directly from payer to merchant. No intermediate holding address exists.

On-chain verification

Every payment is verified against Arcscan before the invoice is marked paid. Unverified payments are never accepted.

Native USDC only

Built on Circle USDC, the regulated stablecoin. No wrapped tokens, no synthetic assets, no bridging risk.

Open and auditable

Contract source is available on Arcscan. Settlement happens on a public testnet ledger that anyone can inspect.
