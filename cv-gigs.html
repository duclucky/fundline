Frequently asked questions

How do I make a CV with AI on Fundline?

Fill in a short form with your skills, experience, and target role. The workflow writes a structured CV from your facts and renders it as a styled page you can save as PDF. It never invents employers or dates.

Where do the freelance gigs come from?

Real, live listings from Freelancer.com and Hacker News (and JSearch when needed), matched to your skills and ranked, each with a real apply or bid link. Upwork and Fiverr are not included because their APIs do not allow it.

How much does it cost?

You pay per run in USDC on Arc, from 0.02 USDC. There is no subscription.

Can I choose a CV template and add a photo?

Yes. The CV opens with a toolbar to switch between several templates, pick an accent color, and upload a profile photo. The photo stays in your browser and is never uploaded to a server. Save to PDF from the browser.

Does it write proposals for the gigs?

Yes. For each top-matched gig it drafts a short tailored proposal opener you can build on before you apply.
